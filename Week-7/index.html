What is a LAN?

A Local Area Network connects devices in a limited area, such as a home, classroom, laboratory or building.

Question: How can 20 computers in one lab communicate?

Why use a switch?

A switch connects wired devices in a LAN and forwards Ethernet frames toward their destination using learned MAC addresses.

What about laptops?

A wireless access point lets Wi-Fi devices connect to a LAN. A LAN may have both wired and wireless devices.

Ethernet or Wi-Fi?

Select a technology and see how a local network connection works.

Ethernet · IEEE 802.3

Common wired LAN technology. Typically uses copper Ethernet cables or fiber, with switches linking devices.

Wi-Fi · IEEE 802.11

Common wireless LAN technology. Radio waves connect clients to an access point.
