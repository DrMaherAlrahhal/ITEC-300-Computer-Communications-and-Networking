03 · Categorize needs

What does the organization actually need?

Classify requirements by category, then test what happens when a requirement is ignored.

Requirement sorting game

Choose the most appropriate category.

Requirement categories

Users & services

People, devices, applications, and coverage

Performance

Speed, capacity, delay, reliability

Security

Access controls, segmentation, protection

Constraints

Budget, building layout, expansion, maintenance

Remember: “We need Wi-Fi 6” is a technology preference. “200 students need reliable wireless access” is a requirement that helps evaluate technologies.
